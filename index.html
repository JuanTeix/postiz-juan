Postiz Juan

A personal, self-hosted tool for scheduling social media posts.

Postiz Juan is a private installation of the open-source scheduler Postiz, run by its owner on their own computer. It is used only to schedule and publish the owner's own content to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok.

What it does

  • Lets the account owner sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorize access to their own account.
  • Uploads and publishes videos the owner chose, at the time the owner scheduled them (Content Posting API).
  • Shows basic profile information and statistics of the connected account inside the tool.

Who can use it

This is not a public service. There is no public sign-up and no third-party users; only accounts belonging to the owner are connected.
