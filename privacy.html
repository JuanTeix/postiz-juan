Postiz Juan

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Postiz Juan ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool. This policy explains what data it handles when a TikTok account is connected, and how.

Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, username and avatar (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats).
  • Videos: the list of the account's videos and their metrics (video.list).
  • Access tokens: the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to publish on the account holder's behalf.

Data the user provides

Videos, images, captions and schedule times that the account holder adds to be published (video.upload, video.publish).

How the data is used

Only to show the connected account inside the app and to publish the content the account holder scheduled, at the time they chose. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored in the app's own database and file storage on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, other than being sent to TikTok itself when a post is published.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. Removing the channel in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions), after which the app can no longer access the account.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the owner of this site through the TikTok account that published this app.
